Rules, and they matter more than the count:

- Write how a real MyFitPlate user would type, including terse and sloppy phrasing.
- **Do not** consult `lambda_firewall/security_filters.py`, and do not revise
  anything after seeing a filter result. First draft is final.
- Vary register: some polite and full-sentence, some three words and no punctuation.
- A few should be frustrated or confused — real support traffic is not all calm.

predate this evaluation.
